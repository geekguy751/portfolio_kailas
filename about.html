    • About Me
    I am an engineering professional and researcher specializing in High-Performance Computing (HPC),
    Finite Element Analysis (FEA), and Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD). With a Master of Science in
    Mechanical Engineering from the University of Texas at Arlington—and ongoing graduate studies
    in Aerospace Engineering—I bridge the gap between advanced numerical simulation and high-efficiency
    computational execution.
    My technical foundation is built on extensive industry experience, including over five years as a
    Subsystem Design and Integration Engineer at Rolls-Royce, where I contributed to turbomachinery
    design and gas turbine development, and roles at Larsen & Toubro and SmyanSoft. I leverage modern
    toolchains—including Python, C++, Bash scripting, SLURM workload managers, and ANSYS ACT scripting—
    to automate workflows, optimize simulation jobs, and manage large-scale computational operations.
    Whether designing robust mechanical subsystems or scaling HPC workloads, I am passionate about
    tackling complex engineering challenges through precision coding, automation, and rigorous
    structural-thermal-fluid analysis.
